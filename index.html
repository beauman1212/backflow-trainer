The practical isn't graded on whether you know what a check valve does — it's graded on whether you do every step in the right order, at the right speed, without being told. This rig makes you do exactly that.

Which edition? Arizona's rule (A.A.C. R18-4-215) still names the USC Manual 9th Edition and says "no future editions." Every body that actually proctors in Arizona — ABPA, CA-NV AWWA, ASSE — teaches and grades the 10th Edition. This trainer drills the 10th. Where the two differ, the step is flagged 9th ≠ 10th so you know it's a real trap.
